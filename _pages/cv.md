Work experience
======
* 10/2024 - Present: Economist II, Amazon Web Services - Marketing
  * Science lead for AWS's multi-touch attribution system, owning the solution from problem formulation and model development (Markov chain and regression-based models calibrated to experimental evidence) through downstream prioritization and targeting recommendations for AWS's go-to-market initiatives.
  * Set the causal measurement strategy for AWS Marketing's owned channels, leading cross-team design and implementation of a framework combining RCTs and quasi-experimental methods, expanding coverage from 4 channels to 15.

* 10/2023 - 10/2024: Economist II, Amazon.com - Worldwide Stores
  * Owned end-to-end development of a double machine learning (DML) measurement solution quantifying how negative customer experiences on Amazon.com change customers' shopping behavior; findings informed remediation strategies that protected roughly $85MM in revenue.

* 08/2022 - 10/2023: Economist I, Amazon.com - Worldwide Stores
  * Designed and executed causal evaluations of product quality interventions, translating technical findings into actionable recommendations for product, operations, and customer service partners.

* 09/2021 - 07/2022: Postdoctoral Research Scientist, George Washington University
  * Designed and analyzed RCTs evaluating group-based interpersonal therapy on mental health and human capital outcomes among at-risk adolescent girls (StrongMinds Uganda; BRAC Uganda ELA program).
  * Directed third-round data collection for GAGE, a multi-country longitudinal study on gendered adolescent development across low- and middle-income countries.

* 08/2021 - 10/2021: Consultant, Catholic Relief Services
  * Provided technical guidance on applied microeconomic methods for evaluating smallholder farmer initiatives in Ethiopia, informing evidence-based strategies to scale the program.

* 08/2015 - 07/2021: Research Assistant, University of Florida
  * Applied econometric and machine learning methods to research projects in political economy, education, and health economics.
